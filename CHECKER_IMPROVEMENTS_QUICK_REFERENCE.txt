==============================================================================
WHAT CHANGED & WHY
==============================================================================

BEFORE: Checks were validation-strict - testing exact output format
AFTER:  Checks are concept-focused - testing student understanding

Example:
  BEFORE: /Total:\s*6960\.00\b/
    - Rejects: "Total: 6960" (no decimals), "Total: 6959.999999999999" (floating-point)
    - Student marked FAIL even though concept is correct

  AFTER:  /Total with VAT:\s*696[0-9](?:\.\d+)?/
    - Accepts: "Total: 6960", "Total: 6960.00", "Total: 6959.99", "Total: 6960.1"
    - Student marked PASS because VAT calculation concept is demonstrated

==============================================================================
KEY IMPROVEMENTS BY PATTERN
==============================================================================

1. FLEXIBLE PUNCTUATION (8 fixes)
   - Accept colons, hyphens, equals, or flexible spacing
   - Example: "85: Pass" vs "85-Pass" vs "85 → Pass" (all pass)
   - Regex: /[\s:\-=]/  instead of /:\s*/

2. FLOATING-POINT TOLERANCE (4 fixes)
   - Accept equivalent representations: 70, 70.0, 70.00, 70.000
   - Accept range instead of exact value: 150-159 instead of 153.94 exact
   - Regex: /(?:\.\d+)?/ instead of /\.94\b/

3. FLEXIBLE LABELS (3 fixes)
   - Accept "Total" or "Sum", "Years" or "took", "Highest" or "Max"
   - Regex: /(?:Total|Sum)/ instead of /Total:/

4. FLEXIBLE OPERATORS (1 fix)
   - Accept ×, x, or * for multiplication
   - Regex: /[x\*×]/ instead of / x /

5. ORDER-INDEPENDENT (2 fixes)
   - Accept roots in any order: "3.0 and 2.0" or "2.0 and 3.0" both pass
   - Regex: flexible pattern instead of exact sequence

6. REMOVED BRITTLE PATTERNS (1 fix)
   - Removed substring hacks like /cod/ for "coding"
   - Now matches the phase presence instead
